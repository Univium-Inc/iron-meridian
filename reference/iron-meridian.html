DEPLOYMENT PHASE

Position your forces west of the river. Drag to select, right-click to place. Alpha, Bravo and Charlie crossings are the objectives: hold more of them than the enemy to bleed their operational strength.

ORDERS REFERENCE

LMB / dragSelect unit / box select. Double-click selects all of a type on screen.
RMBMove. On an enemy: focus fire. Works while paused.
A + clickAttack-move. With artillery selected, bombards the target point.
SHalt selected units.
F + clickCall fixed-wing airstrike (75s cooldown). Enemy air defense can down the jet.
Ctrl + 1..5Assign control group. 1..5 recalls it. E selects the whole army.
SpacePause. Issue orders freely while time is stopped.
Arrows / edge / wheelPan and zoom the camera. Alt + drag or middle mouse also pans.
Doctrine notes
FlankingShots into a vehicle's side or rear arc hit much harder. Watch your facing.
CoverInfantry in forests and towns takes less damage and is spotted far later.
SuppressionFire degrades accuracy and can pin infantry even without kills.
MoraleBattered units rout toward friendly lines. They may rally if given room.
ArtilleryImpact zones are telegraphed to both sides. Displace or die. Friendly fire is real.
Air defenseSPAA hard-counters helicopters and can abort airstrikes.
